## Context

Managed databases are among the most expensive line items in an AWS bill, and clusters tend to
stay provisioned long after their real usage changes.

## What I built

Automation on **AWS Lambda** that optimizes how RDS clusters and databases are managed.

## Impact

- **60% reduction** in RDS instance costs.
